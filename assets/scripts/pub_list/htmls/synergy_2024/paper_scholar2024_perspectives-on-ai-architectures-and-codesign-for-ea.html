Perspectives on AI Architectures and Codesign for Earth System Predictability
    Maruti K Mudunuru, James Ang, Mahantesh Halappanavar, Simon D Hammond, Maya B Gokhale, James C Hoe, Tushar Krishna, Sarat Sreepathi, Matthew R Norman, Ivy B Peng, Philip W Jones Artificial Intelligence for the Earth Systems
    Abstract Paper
    ai systems algorithms

    Abstract

    Presents an AI co-design paradigm for earth system predictability using an iterative model experimentation (ModEx) workflow that links AI-driven hypotheses with model parameterization and testing.
