SDQ: Sparse decomposed quantization for LLM inference
    Geonhwa Jeong, Po-An Tsai, Stephen W Keckler, Tushar Krishna arXiv preprint arXiv:2406.13868
    Abstract Paper
    llm sparsity quantization

    Abstract

    This work introduces SDQ, a method combining structured sparsity and quantization to improve efficient LLM inference with high compute/memory throughput and minimal quality loss.
