HARP: A Taxonomy for Heterogeneous and Hierarchical Processors for Mixed-reuse Workloads
    Raveesh Garg, Michael Pellauer, Tushar Krishna arXiv preprint arXiv:2502.13113
    Abstract Paper
    accelerators dataflow simulation algorithms

    Abstract

    HARP proposes a taxonomy for heterogeneous, hierarchical accelerators for mixed-reuse tensor workloads and evaluates the impact of heterogeneity using a Timeloop-based mapping and cost-modeling approach.
