Enabling unstructured sparse acceleration on structured sparse accelerators
    Geonhwa Jeong, Po-An Tsai, Abhimanyu R Bambhaniya, Stephen W Keckler, Tushar Krishna Proceedings of Machine Learning and Systems
    Abstract Paper
    sparsity accelerators ai systems

    Abstract

    Presents an approximation and software framework to execute unstructured sparse DNNs efficiently on structured-sparsity accelerators, improving energy-delay-product and speed on real hardware.
