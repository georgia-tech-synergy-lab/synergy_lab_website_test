Enhancing scalability and performance in influence maximization with optimized parallel processing
    Hanjiang Wu, Huan Xu, Joongun Park, Jesmin Jahan Tithi, Fabio Checconi, Jordi Wolfson-Pou, Fabrizio Petrini, Tushar Krishna SC24-W: Workshops of the International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage and Analysis
    Abstract Paper
    simulation

    Abstract

    Presents EFFICIENTIMM, a NUMA-aware and dynamically load-balanced parallelization approach to improve the scalability and performance of the Influence Maximization via Martingales (IMM) benchmark on multi-core CPUs.
