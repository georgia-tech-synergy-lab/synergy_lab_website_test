Cosmic: Enabling full-stack co-design and optimization of distributed machine learning systems
    Aditi Raju, Jared Ni, William Won, Changhai Man, Srivatsan Krishnan, Srinivas Sridharan, Amir Yazdanbakhsh, Tushar Krishna, Vijay Janapa Reddi arXiv preprint arXiv:2505.15020
    Abstract Paper
    distributed ai simulation systems

    Abstract

    COSMIC is a full-stack environment for end-to-end simulation and agent-based design space exploration that co-designs distributed transformer ML systems for significant cross-stack performance gains.
