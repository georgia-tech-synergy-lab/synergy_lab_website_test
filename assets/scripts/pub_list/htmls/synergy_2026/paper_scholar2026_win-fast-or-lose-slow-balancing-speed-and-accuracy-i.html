Win fast or lose slow: Balancing speed and accuracy in latency-sensitive decisions of llms
    Hao Kang, Qingru Zhang, Han Cai, Weiyuan Xu, Tushar Krishna, Yilun Du, Tsachy Weissman Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems
    Abstract Paper
    ai systems

    Abstract

    Presents latency–quality trade-offs for LLM-based real-time decisions using new HFT and gaming benchmarks and introduces FPX to adapt model size and quantization under latency constraints.
