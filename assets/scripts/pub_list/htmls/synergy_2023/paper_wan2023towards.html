    Abstract

    The remarkable advancements in artificial intelligence (AI), primarily driven by deep neural networks, have profoundly influenced numerous domains of modern life. However, current challenges—such as unsustainable computational growth, limited robustness, and a lack of explainability—underscore the need for developing next-generation AI systems. Neuro-symbolic AI (NSAI) has emerged as a promising paradigm that fuses neural, symbolic, and probabilistic approaches to enhance interpretability, robustness, and trustworthiness while enabling learning from substantially less data. Recent NSAI systems have demonstrated strong potential in collaborative human–AI scenarios, showcasing advanced reasoning and cognitive capabilities. In this paper, we present a systematic review of recent progress in NSAI, analyzing the performance characteristics and computational operators of representative models. Furthermore, we discuss the key challenges and outline future research directions for advancing NSAI from both system and architectural perspectives.
