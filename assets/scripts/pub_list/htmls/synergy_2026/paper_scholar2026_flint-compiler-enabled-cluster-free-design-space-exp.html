Flint: Compiler Enabled Cluster-Free Design Space Exploration for Distributed ML
    Jinsun Yoo, Meghan Cowan, Zheng Du, Changhai Man, Srinivas Sridharan, Tushar Krishna arXiv preprint arXiv:2604.17550
    Abstract Paper
    distributed systems mlhw simulation

    Abstract

    Flint is a compiler-enabled framework for cluster-free design space exploration of distributed machine learning by extracting workload representations via ML IR and validating them against execution traces.
