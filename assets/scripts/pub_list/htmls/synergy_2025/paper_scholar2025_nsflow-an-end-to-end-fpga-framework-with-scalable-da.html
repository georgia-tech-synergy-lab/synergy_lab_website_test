Nsflow: an end-to-end fpga framework with scalable dataflow architecture for neuro-symbolic AI
    Hanchen Yang, Zishen Wan, Ritik Raj, Joongun Park, Ziwei Li, Ananda Samajdar, Arijit Raychowdhury, Tushar Krishna 2025 62nd ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference (DAC)
    Abstract Paper
    nsymbolic fpga dataflow ai

    Abstract

    NSFlow presents an end-to-end FPGA framework with a scalable dataflow architecture to accelerate neuro-symbolic AI workloads.
