FRED: A Wafer-scale Fabric for 3D Parallel DNN Training
    Saeed Rashidi, William Won, Sudarshan Srinivasan, Puneet Gupta, Tushar Krishna Proceedings of the 52nd Annual International Symposium on Computer Architecture
    Abstract Paper
    distributed accelerators noc ai

    Abstract

    FRED proposes a wafer-scale, nonblocking on-wafer fabric with microswitch-based topology to accelerate collective communication for parallel DNN training.
