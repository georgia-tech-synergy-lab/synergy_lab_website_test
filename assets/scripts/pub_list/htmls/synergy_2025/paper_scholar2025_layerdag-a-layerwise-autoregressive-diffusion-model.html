LayerDAG: A Layerwise Autoregressive Diffusion Model for Directed Acyclic Graph Generation
    Mufei Li, Viraj Shitole, Eli Chien, Changhai Man, Zhaodong Wang, Ying Zhang, Tushar Krishna, Pan Li International Conference on Learning Representations
    Abstract Paper
    ai algorithms simulation

    Abstract

    LayerDAG proposes a layerwise autoregressive diffusion model to generate realistic directed acyclic graphs for computing-system benchmarking.
