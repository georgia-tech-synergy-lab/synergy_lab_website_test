Cello: Co-Designing Schedule and Hybrid Implicit/Explicit Buffer for Complex Tensor Reuse
    Raveesh Garg, Michael Pellauer, Sivasankaran Rajamanickam, Tushar Krishna 2025 IEEE International Parallel and Distributed Processing Symposium (IPDPS)
    Abstract Paper
    distributed

    Abstract

    Presents Cello, a co-designed accelerator with an on-chip hybrid implicit/explicit buffering mechanism and a scheduler to exploit tensor reuse across complex operation DAGs for HPC workloads.
