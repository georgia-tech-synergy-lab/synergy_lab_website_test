LIBRA: Enabling workload-aware multi-dimensional network topology optimization for distributed training of large AI models
    William Won, Saeed Rashidi, Sudarshan Srinivasan, Tushar Krishna 2024 IEEE International Symposium on Performance Analysis of Systems and Software (ISPASS)
    Abstract Paper
    distributed ai systems algorithms

    Abstract

    LIBRA presents a workload-aware framework to optimize multi-dimensional network topologies for reducing communication bottlenecks during distributed training of large AI models.
