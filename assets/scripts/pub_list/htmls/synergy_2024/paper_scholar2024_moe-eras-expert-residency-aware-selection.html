MoE-ERAS: Expert Residency Aware Selection
    Abhimanyu Rajeshkumar Bambhaniya, Sashankh Chengavalli Kumar, Tushar Krishna Machine Learning for Computer Architecture and Systems 2024
    Abstract Paper
    llm systems algorithms

    Abstract

    MoE-ERAS profiles expert activation in mixture-of-experts models and proposes expert residency-aware selection to reduce decoding latency and expert swaps while balancing speed and accuracy.
