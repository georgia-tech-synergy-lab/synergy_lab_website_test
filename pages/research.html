Research

Computer architecture for intelligent systems.

Synergy Lab studies how computation, communication, memory, and software should co-evolve for emerging AI workloads.

Our Approach

Co-design across applications, systems, architecture, and implementation.

We build efficient and scalable platforms by treating data movement, computation, memory behavior, and programmability as one systems problem.

These directions also organize the lab's publication archive, making it possible to follow a topic from early architecture work through current AI systems research.
