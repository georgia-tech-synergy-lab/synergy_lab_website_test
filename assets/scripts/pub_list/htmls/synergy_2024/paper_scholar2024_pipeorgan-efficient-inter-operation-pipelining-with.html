Pipeorgan: Efficient inter-operation pipelining with flexible spatial organization and interconnects
    Raveesh Garg, Hyoukjun Kwon, Eric Qin, Yu-Hsin Chen, Tushar Krishna, Liangzhen Lai arXiv preprint arXiv:2405.01736
    Abstract Paper
    systems

    Abstract

    PipeOrgan proposes a flexible spatial data organization and interconnect strategy to improve inter-operation pipelining efficiency for DNN accelerators.
