First Demonstration of a 200×200 mm2 Glass Substrate for Large Area Packages

    Abstract

    Presents a large-area 200×200 mm2 glass substrate for package-scale systems with UCIe-S 3.0 interfaces, including fabrication and signal/infrastructure simulations to quantify communication scaling benefits.
