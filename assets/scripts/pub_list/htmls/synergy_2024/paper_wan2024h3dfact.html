    Abstract

    Disentangling the attributes of diverse sensory signals is fundamental to human-like perception and reasoning and serves as a cornerstone for higher-order cognitive and neuro-symbolic AI systems. An elegant way to represent this intricate factorization is through high-dimensional holographic vectors, inspired by brain-like vector symbolic architectures (VSAs). However, holographic factorization entails iterative computations involving high-dimensional matrix–vector multiplications and often suffers from non-convergence issues. In this paper, we present H3DFACT, a heterogeneous 3D integrated in-memory compute engine designed to efficiently factorize high-dimensional holographic representations. H3DFACT leverages the computation-in-superposition property of holographic vectors and harnesses the intrinsic stochasticity of memristive-based 3D compute-in-memory architectures. Evaluated on large-scale factorization and perceptual tasks, H3DFACT demonstrates superior performance in both factorization accuracy and operational capacity, achieving up to five orders of magnitude improvement, along with 5.5× higher compute density, 1.2× better energy efficiency, and 5.9× smaller silicon footprint compared to iso-capacity 2D designs.
