Scaling Multi-Node Mixture-of-Experts Inference Using Expert Activation Patterns
    Abhimanyu Bambhaniya, Geonhwa Jeong, Jason Park, Jiecao Yu, Jaewon Lee, Pengchao Wang, Changkyu Kim, Chunqiang Tang, Tushar Krishna arXiv preprint arXiv:2604.23150
    Abstract Paper
    llm distributed ai systems

    Abstract

    Presents workload-aware micro-batching and expert placement strategies for multi-node MoE LLM inference that reduce inter-node all-to-all communication and improve decode latency.
