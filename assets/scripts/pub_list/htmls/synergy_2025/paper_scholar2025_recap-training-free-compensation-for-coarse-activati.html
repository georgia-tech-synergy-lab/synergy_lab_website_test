Recap: Training-free compensation for coarse activation channel pruning in compressed llms
    Mingyu Lee, Akshat Ramachandran, Tushar Krishna Machine Learning for Computer Architecture and Systems 2025
    Abstract Paper
    llm sparsity ai quantization

    Abstract

    This paper introduces RECAP, a training-free method to compensate errors from coarse activation channel pruning in compressed LLMs, improving accuracy across models and sparsity levels.
