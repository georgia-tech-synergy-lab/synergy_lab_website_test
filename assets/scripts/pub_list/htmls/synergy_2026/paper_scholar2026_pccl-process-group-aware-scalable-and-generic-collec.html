PCCL: Process Group-Aware Scalable and Generic Collective Algorithm Synthesizer
    William Won, Kartik Lakhotia, Madhu Kumar, Sudarshan Srinivasan, Tushar Krishna arXiv preprint arXiv:2606.07019
    Abstract Paper

    Abstract

    PCCL synthesizes topology-aware collective communication algorithms for distributed machine learning, accounting for process-group participation and generating arbitrary collective patterns efficiently.
