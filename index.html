Computer architecture and systems research for the next generation of AI.

Publications People

Computer Architecture & Systems

Rethinking how modern computing platforms move and process data.

Led by Prof. Tushar Krishna, Synergy Lab studies computer architecture, interconnection networks, networks-on-chip, and AI/ML systems at Georgia Tech.

Our work connects applications, system software, architecture, CAD, and circuits, with an emphasis on practical tools and cross-layer decisions that reduce the cost of data movement.
