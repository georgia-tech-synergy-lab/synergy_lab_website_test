MicroscopiQ: Accelerating Foundational Models through Outlier-Aware Microscaling Quantization
    Akshat Ramachandran, Souvik Kundu, Tushar Krishna ISCA 2025
    📄 Abstract 📑 Paper 💻 Code

    Abstract

    Quantization of foundational models (FMs) is significantly more challenging than traditional deep neural networks (DNNs) due to the emergence of large magnitude values called outliers. Existing outlier-aware algorithm–architecture co-design techniques either use mixed precision, retaining outliers at high precision but compromising hardware efficiency, or quantize inliers and outliers at the same precision, improving hardware efficiency at the cost of accuracy. To address this mutual exclusivity, we propose MicroScopiQ, a novel co-design technique that leverages pruning to complement outlier-aware quantization. MicroScopiQ retains outliers at higher precision while pruning a certain fraction of the least important weights to distribute the additional outlier bits, ensuring high accuracy along with aligned memory and hardware efficiency. We design a high-throughput, low-overhead accelerator architecture composed of multi-precision integer processing elements and a network-on-chip called ReCoN that efficiently abstracts the complexity of supporting high-precision outliers. Additionally, unlike prior techniques, MicroScopiQ does not assume any locality of outlier weights, enabling applicability to a broad range of foundational models. Extensive experiments across diverse quantization settings demonstrate that MicroScopiQ achieves state-of-the-art quantization accuracy while delivering up to 3× faster inference and 2× lower energy consumption compared to existing alternatives.
