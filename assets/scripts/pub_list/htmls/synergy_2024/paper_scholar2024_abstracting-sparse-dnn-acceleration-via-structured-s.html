Abstracting sparse DNN acceleration via structured sparse tensor decomposition
    Geonhwa Jeong, Po-An Tsai, Abhimanyu R Bambhaniya, Stephen W Keckler, Tushar Krishna CoRR, abs/2403.07953
    Abstract Paper
    sparsity systems algorithms

    Abstract

    Presents a framework that decomposes sparse DNN tensors into hardware-friendly structured sparse forms to enable accelerator-agnostic sparse inference and reduce energy-delay product.
