OckBench: Measuring the Efficiency of LLM Reasoning
    Zheng Du, Hao Kang, Song Han, Tushar Krishna, Ligeng Zhu arXiv preprint arXiv:2511.05722
    Abstract Paper
    llm simulation systems

    Abstract

    OckBench introduces a model- and hardware-agnostic benchmark that evaluates LLM reasoning/coding tasks by measuring both accuracy and token efficiency to expose latency, cost, and energy differences.
