Towards efficient neuro-symbolic ai: From workload characterization to hardware architecture
    Zishen Wan, Che-Kai Liu, Hanchen Yang, Ritik Raj, Chaojian Li, Haoran You, Yonggan Fu, Cheng Wan, Sixu Li, Youbin Kim, Ananda Samajdar, Yingyan Lin, Mohamed Ibrahim, Jan M Rabaey, Tushar Krishna, Arijit Raychowdhury IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Artificial Intelligence
    Abstract Paper
    nsymbolic ai systems

    Abstract

    This paper characterizes neuro-symbolic AI workloads and evaluates candidate hardware architectures to improve efficiency, runtime, and memory behavior.
